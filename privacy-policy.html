Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Phira Studios ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information when you visit phirastudios.com or engage our services.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you contact us, book a call, or subscribe to our newsletter, we may collect:

  • Name and contact details (email, company, optionally phone)
  • Information about your business and project goals
  • Communications you send to us (emails, messages, call notes)

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, we may collect basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring URL, and pages visited. This is used to understand site usage and improve our service.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide our services
  • Send you our newsletter, if you subscribed (you can unsubscribe anytime)
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Cookies and tracking

We may use cookies and similar technologies to understand how visitors use our site and to deliver a smoother experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings — most parts of the site will continue to work.

4. Third-party services

We use trusted third-party services to operate the business, including:

  • Hostinger — website hosting
  • Email providers (e.g., Outlook, newsletter platforms) — for communication
  • Analytics (e.g., Google Analytics) — to understand site traffic
  • Booking tools (e.g., Calendly) — for scheduling calls

These services have their own privacy policies. Information they collect is governed by their terms.

5. Data storage and security

We store your data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined here, or as required by law. We use reasonable security measures to protect your information, though no internet transmission is 100% secure.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR for EU/UK, CCPA for California, etc.), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent (e.g., unsubscribe from emails)
  • Request data portability

To exercise any of these rights, email us at phira.studios@outlook.com.

7. International transfers

As a remote-first studio working with clients globally, data may be transferred and stored across different countries. We ensure such transfers comply with applicable privacy laws.

8. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any revisions. Material changes will be communicated where appropriate.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at phira.studios@outlook.com.
