Case Study

Consistent product imagery across 80+ frames — without a studio.

Eyewear & accessories · United States · AI product photography

The problem. An independent eyewear store had 80+ frames with wildly inconsistent imagery — different lighting, different backgrounds, some with no photos at all. A studio reshoot was quoted at $8,000+ and 6 weeks lead time.

What we did. Built a unified visual system and applied it to every frame — same angle, same lighting, same shadow treatment. Three angles per frame, delivered in two batches over 12 days.

The outcome. The full catalogue (84 frames) now looks like a single, intentional brand. Measurable lifts in add-to-cart rate, time on product page, and Google Shopping click-through.
